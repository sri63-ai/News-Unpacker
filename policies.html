LEGAL & REGULATORY FRAMEWORK

Compliance & Extended Policies

Official Guidelines, Cookies Usage, Fair AI Principles, and Anti-Spam Commitments under Sriram Groups.

1. Cookies & Local Storage Policy

News Unpacker uses essential browser cookies and device local storage (LocalStorage) strictly to ensure uninterrupted, secure service delivery:

  • Session Persistence: To keep you logged in safely across visits without requesting re-authentication every time.
  • Device Usage Quota: To anonymously track the guest free daily limit (5 summaries per device per day) without requiring personal info.
  • Theme & Voice Preferences: To remember your selected speech speed (1.0x, 1.25x, 2.0x) and workspace configurations.

We do not use invasive third-party tracking cookies or sell device data to external advertisers.

2. Anti-Spam & Fair Communications Policy

Sriram Groups strictly adheres to global zero-spam standards:

  • Strict Verification Only: Emails from sriramgroups.help@gmail.com are triggered only for essential functional requests: 6-digit OTP verification, secure password reset links, and active subscription invoices.
  • No Unsolicited Newsletters: We will never spam your inbox with third-party marketing, unverified affiliate offers, or bulk sales promotions.
  • Immediate Opt-Out: Users retain full autonomy to request account data deletion or communication pauses anytime via our helpline.

3. AI Factual Processing & Content Ethics

News Unpacker processes news stories via high-speed neural pipelines designed strictly for factual clarification:

  • Non-Editorialized Outputs: Our engines strip inflammatory media language, hyperbole, and speculative rumors to produce objective, balanced summaries.
  • No Hate Speech or Defamation: Processing text that incites violence, promotes hate speech, or defames individuals is strictly restricted.
  • Source Transparency: The output generated is a synthetic extraction of user-supplied news copy; users should verify original sources for official administrative or legal procedures.

4. Payments, Refunds & Cancellation Policy

Our subscription passes are processed securely through certified Indian payment gateways :

  • Digital Service Activation: Because subscription tokens grant instant, unmetered AI access upon confirmation, subscription fees are generally non-refundable once activated.
  • Failed or Duplicate Charges: In the rare event of a duplicate bank debit or transaction failure, refunds are auto-credited back to the source bank/UPI account within 5 to 7 business days.
