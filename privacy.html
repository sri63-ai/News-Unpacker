News Unpacker Privacy & Safety Policy

Your inputs, news paste data, and analysis workflows are cryptographically isolated. We hold a strict zero-retention guarantee.

🛡️ Absolute Zero-Sharing & Safe System Guarantee

News Unpacker does not sell, lease, monetize, or transfer your input data to any third party, ad broker, or external AI model trainer. Your submitted text exists exclusively in RAM runtime memory and is deleted instantly once synthesis finishes.

1. Non-Retention of Submitted News Data

When you paste text into our summarization, bias-detection, translation, or speech tools:

  • No Hard Disk Archiving: The submitted text is held ephemerally in active memory only for the seconds required to compute the analysis.
  • Instant Destruction: As soon as the bulleted summary or audio stream is returned to your browser, all memory traces are automatically garbage-collected and permanently destroyed.
  • No Search Logging: We do not log what topics, politicians, companies, or articles you choose to analyze.

2. Zero Third-Party Sharing Rules

Unlike conventional media outlets and platforms:

  • We do NOT partner with data brokers or corporate trackers.
  • We do NOT feed your personal reading snippets into public model training datasets.
  • We do NOT build behavioral profiling cookies to track your movements across other internet portals.

3. Local Storage & Client-Side Execution

Settings like audio playback speed (1.5x, 2x) or dark mode preferences are stored locally in your own browser's localStorage. This data never touches our remote servers.
