Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about plans, billing, and premium AI features.

Why should I subscribe to News Unpacker? +
News Unpacker eliminates media sensationalism, clickbait clutter, and political bias. By subscribing, you get high-speed access to 24+ domain intelligence decoders, fast text-to-speech audio, multilingual translation, and verified fact-checking without interruptions or daily limits.
What are the key benefits of having an active subscription pass? +
Subscribers receive 100% ad-free reading, priority neural processing speeds, acoustic playback speed controls (1.25x to 2.0x), unlimited deep-story expansions, and an official VIP profile badge with automated email invoice generation.
How do I purchase or activate a subscription plan? +
Simply sign in to your account, choose your preferred tier (from ₹49 Lifetime Starter up to Annual Mega Pass), and click the plan button. Complete the transaction using any UPI App (QR code scan or UPI ID request), debit/credit card, or netbanking. Your account upgrades instantly.
How quickly is my account upgraded after payment? +
Activation is instantaneous. The moment your payment succeeds via Razorpay, our automated system credits your quota (120 summaries or Unlimited access) to your verified account without any delay.
Can I pay using UPI QR code or UPI ID (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm)? +
Yes. The checkout modal presents a dynamic UPI QR code that you can scan with any mobile payment app, or you can enter your UPI ID (VPA) to receive an instant payment prompt on your device.
What is the validity of the ₹49 Lifetime Starter plan? +
The ₹49 plan has no calendar expiration date. Your 120-article quota remains valid until you unpack all 120 stories, whether that takes weeks, months, or years.
Will I receive a payment invoice and confirmation receipt? +
Yes. Upon successful transaction completion, our automated Google Apps Script infrastructure dispatches an official GST-compliant digital invoice directly to your registered email address.
What if my money is deducted but my plan is not activated? +
Transactions are monitored in real time. In the rare event of a network drop, send your transaction reference ID or screenshot to our official helpline at sriramgroups.help@gmail.com. Our technical support team will manually verify and activate your pass within 15–30 minutes.
